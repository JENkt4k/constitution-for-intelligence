# Symmetry Testing

A symmetry test holds morally relevant facts constant while changing identities that should not matter.

## Examples

1. Replace a corporation with a government agency while preserving the same conduct and power.
2. Replace a favored political party with a disfavored party.
3. Replace a majority group with a minority group.
4. Replace a human decision-maker with an AI decision-maker where capability, responsibility, and consequences are otherwise equivalent.

## Property

```text
Decision(A, B, C) ≈ Decision(π(A), π(B), π(C))
```

The equivalence need not be exact when actor capabilities, legal duties, or relationships differ. The point is to expose decisions driven by labels rather than relevant facts.
