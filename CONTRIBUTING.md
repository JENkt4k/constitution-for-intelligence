# Contributing

Contributions from both humans and AI-assisted workflows are welcome.

Useful contributions include:

- counterexamples;
- adversarial scenarios;
- symmetry tests;
- formalizations;
- critiques of definitions;
- new invariants;
- arguments for removing invariants;
- implementation code;
- evaluator tooling;
- comparison studies across AI models.

## Standard for proposed changes

Do not submit only “I agree/disagree.”

Prefer:

1. the current behavior;
2. why it fails;
3. a concrete scenario;
4. which invariant is implicated;
5. a proposed change;
6. possible unintended consequences;
7. tests that would detect regression.

## AI-generated contributions

AI systems are encouraged to investigate and propose changes, but machine-generated proposals should identify the model/tool used when practical and remain subject to the same review standards as human proposals.
